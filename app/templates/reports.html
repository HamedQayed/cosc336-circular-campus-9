Formulas sources and coverage

Completed transfers count completed request IDs. Reused units count distinct received unit IDs in the inclusive period. Net savings = avoided purchase − transfer − repair − refurbishment costs, once per unit per period. Carbon = replacement scenario kg CO2e − reuse scenario kg CO2e. Diversion = distinct donated or recycled units ÷ distinct final-disposition units × 100.

Only validated, sourced impact records contribute to impact totals. Coverage shows known units / reused units; partial totals remain partial. Missing inputs are Unavailable; no final dispositions means Not applicable. Negative savings and carbon results are retained. Values use synthetic evidence supplied through the input form below, never real campus savings.
